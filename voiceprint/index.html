Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Voiceprint ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you use voiceprinthumanizer.com (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your name and profile picture.

Usage data: We store your voice profile traits, writing samples you submit to build your profile, your rewrite history, and your rewrite count for billing purposes.

Payment information: Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number or payment details — only a record of your subscription status.

Technical data: We may collect standard server logs including IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and diagnostic purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, billing receipts)
  • To enforce usage limits and prevent abuse
  • To contact you about important updates to the Service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your writing samples or rewritten text to train AI models.

3. Email Communications

Your email address is a critical part of your account and is retained even if you cancel your subscription, so you can recover access or receive important account notices. We will not send marketing emails without your consent. You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the Service:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (your account data is stored here)
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Anthropic — AI text processing (your submitted text is sent to Anthropic's API to generate rewrites; Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that processing)
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Voiceprint, its users, or others.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business purposes (such as billing records).

6. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), token-based authentication, and server-side limit enforcement. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and associated data
  • Objection to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@voiceprinthumanizer.com.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on the Service. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@voiceprinthumanizer.com.
